Seasons

The infinite season always runs and holds the counted results of its Liga and Clanliga members. Custom seasons count only the results between their dates, of players who joined the season (/ladder join SEASON in the game, or the Members page), and start everyone again; several may run at once. Also s3lobby ladder season on the command line.
