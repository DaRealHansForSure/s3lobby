Members

Players take part only in the ladders they joined: Liga, Clanliga and each custom season one by one. A ladder game counts in a ladder only when every player in it had joined that ladder before the match; otherwise nothing is stored for it. Players join with /ladder join in the game; here, and with s3lobby ladder join, admins do it for them. A player who leaves keeps their results for their opponents and starts at the start rating when joining again.
